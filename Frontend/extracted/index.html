Assessment formats

Four Formats. One Standard of Proof.

Every format measures a different layer of understanding — and every submission generates evidence.

MCQs

Knowledge check — does the employee know the law, the definitions, the process?

Proof Scored response + timestamp per question

Fill in the Blanks

Recall validation — can they reproduce thresholds, timelines and committee rules unprompted?

Proof Exact response trace, matched against the Act

Case Studies

Judgment evaluation — weighted options score the quality of the decision, not just correctness.

Proof Judgment score with option-weight breakdown

Simulations

Real workplace decisions — live scenarios where each choice carries a decision-impact rating.

Proof Decision path + impact rating, audit-logged

Certification logic

Strict Thresholds. No Grey Zones.

Certification is computed, not granted. The same two rules apply to every organisation on the platform.

Individual Certification

Threshold · 80%

An employee is certified only when their overall assessment score reaches 80% or above.

0%50%100%
Proof Digital certificate with score, scenario IDs and audit log reference

Organisation Readiness

Threshold · 95% of staff

An organisation becomes POSH Ready only when 95% of its employees are certified with 80%+.

0%50%100%
Proof POSH Ready status record, visible on the admin dashboard and in the audit pack

The three output states

State 1✕ Not Certified

Score below 80%. Re-training is triggered and a re-attempt is scheduled.

State 2✓ Certified Individual

Score 80%+. Certificate issued with evidence trail.

State 3★ Organisation POSH Ready

95% of employees certified. Audit booking unlocked.
